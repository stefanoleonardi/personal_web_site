Stefano Leonardi is Full Professor of Computer Science at the Department of Computer, Control, and Management Engineering “Antonio Ruberti” (DIAG), Sapienza University of Rome, where he leads the Algorithms and Data Science Research Group.

His research spans the theoretical and practical foundations of **Online and Approximation Algorithms**, **Online Learning**, **Algorithmic Game Theory**, **Mechanism Design**, and **Economics & Computation**.
