### 7-Part Syllabus
1. **Introduction to Algorithmic Game Theory & Mechanism Design**: Foundational concepts, strategies, computational economics.
2. **Algorithmic Mechanism Design**: Single Item, Multi-parameter, Revenue Maximizing, Sponsored Search, Spectrum Auctions.
3. **Market Equilibria**: Walrasian Equilibria, Stable Matching, Kidney Exchange, House Allocation.
4. **Computational Game Theory & Price of Anarchy**: Solution concepts, Congestion Games, Selfish Routing, Potential Games.
5. **Computational Social Choice**: Voting systems, rank aggregation, fair division.
6. **Online Decision-Making & Optimal Stopping**: Secretary Problems, Prophet Inequalities.
7. **Learning in Games & Markets**: Learning Equilibria, Experts algorithms, Multi-Armed Bandits, Market dynamics.
