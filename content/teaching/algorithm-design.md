### 11-Topic Syllabus
1. Overview of Algorithm Analysis [Kleinberg & Tardos, Ch. 2.1–2.4]
2. Stable Matching [Kleinberg & Tardos, Ch. 1.1, 1.2]
3. Network Flow: Ford-Fulkerson, Capacity Scaling, Shortest Augmenting Paths [Ch. 7.1–7.3]
4. Perfect Matching, Circulations, Bipartite Matching [Ch. 1.1, 7.5, 7.7]
5. Greedy Algorithms [Ch. 4]
6. Dynamic Programming [Ch. 4.1, 4.4, 6.1, 6.2, 6.4, 6.8]
7. Basics of Complexity & NP-Completeness [Ch. 8]
8–9. Basics of Approximation Algorithms [Vazirani, Ch. 1–3, 8.1, 8.2]
10. LP-Based Approximation Algorithms [Vazirani, Ch. 12]
11. Basics of Randomized Algorithms [Mitzenmacher & Upfal, Ch. 5]

### Main Textbook
- J. Kleinberg, E. Tardos, *Algorithm Design*, Addison Wesley, 2005.
