### Project Overview

**AMDROMA** (*Algorithmic and Mechanism Design Research in Online MArkets*) was an **ERC Advanced Grant** project funded by the European Research Council.

The project focused on the theoretical and empirical foundations of online market design, tackling critical problems in:
- Online internet advertising and auction design
- Two-sided matching markets and platform economics
- Autobidding dynamics and budget management
- Fairness and information asymmetry in digital marketplaces
