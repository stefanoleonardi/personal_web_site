Biography & Research Overview

Stefano Leonardi is Full Professor of Computer Science at the Department of Computer, Control, and Management Engineering “Antonio Ruberti” (DIAG), Sapienza University of Rome, where he leads the Algorithms and Data Science Research Group. His research spans the theoretical and practical foundations of Online and Approximation Algorithms, Online Learning, Algorithmic Game Theory, Mechanism Design, and Economics & Computation.

Master's Degree (II Semestre) A.A. 2025/2026

Economics and Computation

Target Programs: Master's Degree in Data Science / Computer Science Engineering / Management Engineering / Artificial Intelligence and Robotics

Wed 12:00 – 15:00
Instructors: Prof. Stefano Leonardi
Dr. Johannes Brüstle
Classroom Code: gefl77uc
Open Google Classroom Portal
1. Introduction to Algorithmic Game Theory & Mechanism Design

Foundational concepts of game theory, strategies, and computational economic modeling.

2. Algorithmic Mechanism Design

Single Item Auctions, Multi-parameter Mechanism Design, Revenue Maximizing Auctions, Sponsored Search Auctions, Spectrum Auctions.

3. Market Equilibria

Walrasian Equilibria, Stable Matching, Kidney Exchange, House Allocation mechanisms.

4. Computational Game Theory & Price of Anarchy

Games and solution concepts, Congestion Games, Selfish Routing, Price of Anarchy, Potential Games.

5. Computational Social Choice

Voting systems, rank aggregation, fair division protocols.

6. Online Decision-Making & Optimal Stopping Theory

Secretary Problems, Prophet Inequalities under online constraints.

7. Learning in Games & Markets

Learning Equilibria in Games, Experts algorithms, Multi-Armed Bandits, Learning dynamics in digital markets.
